# Contributor licensing policy

Contributions to Wiii's original core material must be authorized for
distribution under the MIT License. No separate contributor agreement for
commercial dual licensing is required. Contributors retain their copyright;
opening a pull request does not assign it.

Contributions to `sdk/` must be authorized for distribution under
Apache-2.0. Third-party code must retain its original license and attribution
and must not be presented as an original contribution.

Contributors must represent that they have the right to submit the work,
including employer permission where applicable. Maintainers must not merge a
contribution when its provenance or licensing authority is unclear.

This policy is an operational gate for license compatibility and provenance.
