# Art direction — Wiii against Drift

Status: Active for `site/anime/`

The page is a battle, not a brochure. Wiii is the logo made human. Drift is an
original rival: an automation that changes everything without asking. Every
Wiii move is a real product fact. Every Drift move is the failure that fact
prevents.

Original characters © HoLiLiHu. No third-party characters, footage, or fonts.
Onomatopoeia is original: シュンッ, ズバッ, ピタッ, トンッ, ワァッ, シーン.
The climax mark is 囲.

## Allegiance

- Ink `#0B0B0D`, paper `#F3EEE3`, charcoal from the logo, signal red `#E0261F`
  only for Drift and impacts.
- Cream type on ink for Wiii. Red type for Drift. Charcoal type on the cream
  inside Kakoi and on Still.
- Anton for display, Yuji Syuku (subset) for brush marks, Instrument Serif for
  the quiet lines.

## Acts

0. Awaken. The spark in the eye is the visitor's cursor.
1. Intrusion. Drift's cursor-fingers reach for the pointer and for the work.
2. Clash. Impact frame, then Project / Session / Tools as torn panels. The
   work stays in the folder.
3. Observe. Time stops. Observation is not control.
4. Neko. Taking control waits. An interrupted action is not repeated when its
   outcome is unknown, so the second strike shatters.
5. Kakoi. The scarf is the ring. One harness rule at a time. The pointer is
   freed.
6. Still. Computer is optional. Wiii Service is optional. Neither is required
   to begin.
7. Return. The spark goes back to the cursor. Link to the repository.

## Interaction

Click or tap strikes at the pointer. Drift's body and cursor-tips are hit
targets. A miss leaves ink. Three quick hits are a finisher. Hold and release
dashes. Scroll speed smears. Stopping holds the pose. Reduced motion is the
same art and copy as a stacked comic, with no inversion and no shake.

## Pictures

Keyframes are cropped in and covered with grain, halftone, and ink. They are
served as AVIF and WebP. The transparent cutouts are the layered fighters.
