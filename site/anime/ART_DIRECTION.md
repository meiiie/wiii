# Wiii — Cause and Effect

Art direction for the long-scroll showcase. Original piece. The visual language
borrows impact frames, ink, speed, smear, and stillness. It does not borrow
characters, names, logos, footage, or type from any existing anime.

## Premise

**Absolute cause and effect.** One spark is the cause of every later motion.
The scroll is a single chain: each act ends by becoming the next act's first
frame. The last frame is the opening spark again, so the piece is a loop even
though the page does not hijack the scrollbar.

The spark is the same idea as the Wiii brand sting (rest, flight, fold, mark,
impact, shock, sweep, contract). The site does not replay that film as video.
Scroll compresses its beats, then spends the middle of the episode on the
product the sting exists to introduce.

## What we studied, and what we refused

Language only, from action-animation grammar and from long-form scroll sites:

- An impact is a cut, not a blur. One bright frame, then a held drawing of
  speed lines, debris, and a word. It is not a strobe.
- A smear is the form stretched along its velocity between keys.
- Speed and ma alternate. Density spikes, then the frame freezes (grain, no
  new action) so the hit can land.
- A brush stroke is allowed to change jobs: path, then edge, then border.
- A "domain" here is an original idea: the tail closes and the workspace
  inside it is the only place the work is allowed to happen. The name on the
  page is **Kakoi (囲)** — an enclosure. It is not a borrowed technique name.
- Award-site craft we actually use: pinned scrubbing, one motif that survives
  every scene, type larger than a column, a horizontal passage inside a
  vertical scroll, a shader atmosphere under the type.

Refused: any existing anime's cast, crests, captions, title type, or color
scripts. Onomatopoeia is ordinary Japanese sound-language, placed for this
story: シュンッ, クルリ, トンッ, ズバッ, シーン, ワァッ, ピタッ.

## Type

- **Inter 650**, tight tracking: the product name, matching the wordmark face.
- **Instrument Serif** italic: sentences that should feel spoken after a cut.
- **Instrument Sans**: explanatory copy.
- **DM Mono**: timecode, cues, indexes. The page is also an exposure sheet.
- **Dela Gothic One**, subset to the characters this page actually sets:
  display kana and the kanji 原因と結果, 囲, 静, 間.

The lockup itself is the official SVG. Motion may delay its entrance. It may
not redraw it.

## Motion rules

- The spark is sprung (inertia). Scroll is smoothed. Hits are one-shots when
  a beat is crossed downward, so they read as cuts rather than scrubs.
- Fast, then stop, then burst. Plateaus are intentional.
- Impacts: one white frame (~70ms), a held ink frame with speed lines and a
  red word (~320ms), a short discrete shake, a chromatic edge. Never more
  than one flash per beat, and beats are separated by a long scroll.
- Reduced motion: no pin, no shader loop, no flash, no shake. The same words
  in document order, on ink and paper.
- Neko stays on model. Peek, Mochi, Nap, and Tilt are poses of one companion.
  Ivory body, graphite capsule eyes, cocoa tail. No mouth, whiskers, paws,
  pupils, or costume. Tilt may carry one Sky point outside the face. Error
  is never a face; this page has no error mascot.

## Beat sheet

Global scroll is one episode. Times in the cold open are the brand sting's
own cue labels, compressed into the pin.

### 01 Spark — cold open

| Progress | Sting cue | Picture | Causes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.00–0.08 | 0.00 spark at rest | A point of milk on ink. Grain. Exposure-sheet corners. | The point is the only light, so the eye is already tracking the cause. |
| 0.08–0.30 | 1.25 flight traces tail | The point runs a rising arc. Thin stroke. シュンッ. | The arc is the path the tail will occupy. |
| 0.30–0.48 | 2.50 fold wraps | The stroke thickens and curls into an oval. クルリ. | The curl is the fold. An abstract volume sits where the mark will land. |
| 0.48–0.70 | 3.58 cat + W | The official on-dark mark and the Inter word **Wiii** resolve as one lockup. The fold fades under it. | The stroke has become the mark. The spark is still loose. |
| 0.70–0.80 | 5.35 impact | The spark flies into the tittle of the last i. トンッ. White frame, speed lines, shake. | The dot of the name is the hit. |
| 0.80–1.00 | 5.75 shockwave | A milk ring expands from that dot. | The ring leaves the pin and becomes the border weight of the next act. |

### 02 Title — light sweep

The sting's sweep (8.70s) is a Sky/milk band that crosses the frame once.
Behind it, the name is set huge, with 原因と結果 set vertically.

| End of this act | Causes |
| --- | --- |
| The ring has faded, but the milk line-weight remains. A diagonal blade starts at the lower right. | The blade is the panel border that has left the page. |

### 03 Work — rising action

A manga spread, gutters thicker than a UI card, one panel rotated off the grid.
The blade finishes its cut as the first panel wipes open. ズバッ sits across
the gutter, not inside a safe padding box.

| Panel | Fact on the page | Causal job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project | Choose a project folder. The model is configured through Neko Core. | First panel border is the shock's leftover edge. |
| Session | Conversations stay with the folder and can be reopened. | Its top rule continues the previous panel's rule. |
| Tools | Công cụ: files, changes, browser, terminal, optional Computer. | The gutter is the same stroke, turned vertical. |
| Still | Observation is not control. | The panel is paper inserted into ink: a frozen frame. シーン. Grain rises. The scroll keeps moving; the picture holds. |

A local project does not require a Wiii Service account. Vietnamese is the
primary interface language. Both lines are on the spread, not in a footnote
invented to fill space.

**Handoff:** the still panel's right edge extends into the blade that cuts
the next act open.

### 04 Neko

Paper. The blade's cut reveals it. Intro, then a sideways move through four
poses of the same Neko:

- Peek — present, listening, ready
- Mochi — comfortable, complete, available
- Nap — resting, without disappearing
- Tilt — curious, checking, needs attention (Sky point outside the face)

Neko Core is the default for new local work. Wiii does not silently substitute
another installed agent when Neko is unavailable.

**Handoff:** the tail's curl whips into a ring. Crossing into Kakoi, the paper
outside that ring falls away to ink. ワァッ. The ring is the tail.

### 05 Kakoi — climax

囲. Inside the ring the surface stays paper: the granted workspace. Outside is
ink and radial lines. The official mark is in the ring at the start, then yields
to the rules as the ring grows past the frame:

- Project and session, reopened rather than recreated
- Tools beside the conversation
- A missing harness is not a failed check (Tổng quan → Quản lý harness)
- An interrupted action is not repeated when its outcome is unknown
- Computer is optional: a persistent Linux desktop through Docker Desktop, its
  own browser profile, experimental, a same-user workstation
- Wiii Service is optional and separate from local desktop work

When the ring swallows the viewport, the picture holds. Ma. The speed lines die.

**Handoff:** being inside the ring *is* the next act. The background does not
change. Only the voice does.

### 06 Still — denouement

One sentence at a time, enormous, on paper:

1. Observation is not control.
2. An outcome you cannot see is not a success.
3. Taking control cancels Wiii-owned queued input and waits. It does not undo
   an email already sent, and it does not stop every program inside the desktop.

ピタッ on the settle. Small type for the rest of the boundary: only granted
folders belong in the Computer; unconfirmed cleanup stays blocked.

**Handoff:** the spark, now cocoa on paper, opens a well of ink. The well
grows until the paper is gone.

### 07 Return — credits

Colophon, then the copy fades. The frame that remains is the opening frame:
ink, grain, one spark at rest, same screen position.

## Website rules

- Real copy only, from the README, the changelog, the brand system, and the
  published GitHub release. No invented metrics, quotes, or features.
- Self-hosted type. No analytics, no third-party pixels.
- Heavy pieces (scroll engine, shader, synthesized audio) load after first
  paint. Audio is off until the visitor asks, and it is synthesized here.
- Mobile keeps the chain: stacked beats instead of a sideways track, same
  impacts, shorter pins.
- The worker is `wiii-anime-site`, custom domain `wiii.holilihu.online`.

## Self-critique bar

Each pass, the weakest act is the one that could be mistaken for a template
section. The test: if you covered the words, would the picture still have
speed, a stop, and a cause?
